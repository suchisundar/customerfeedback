    How the score works
    
        
    Each complaint theme gets six component scores between 0 and 1. The impact score is the weighted sum, scaled to 100:
    
        
    
        
    Revenue uses the square root of contract value so one large account does not swamp everything else. Breadth counts distinct accounts, not comments, so a customer who writes in twice is counted once.
    
        
    Priority tiers
    
        
      
        
      What counts as a complaint
      
        
      An item is treated as praise when its rating is 4 or 5 and the text makes no request ("need", "want", "would", "please", "cannot" and similar). Praise is kept out of the ranking and shown as strengths to protect. Items are grouped by the dataset's product_area; when that column is missing, a keyword classifier reads the text.
      
      
      
      
      
        
      The prompt this app implements
      
        
      
          You are helping a product manager decide which customer problems to investigate and address first.
          The goal is not merely to summarize the comments. The goal is to make product priorities clearer.
          Use the customer comment, rating, customer segment, product area, and cancellation status when available.
          Do not assume that frequency alone determines importance. Consider severity, affected segment, and possible business impact.
